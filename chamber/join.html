NP Membership Benefits

Designed for nonprofit organizations.

  • No membership fee.
  • Access to chamber networking activities.
  • Community event participation.
  • Access to chamber resources.

Bronze Membership Benefits

A great starting point for local businesses.

  • Business directory listing.
  • Networking opportunities.
  • Access to selected chamber events.
  • Member news and updates.

Silver Membership Benefits

More opportunities to promote your business.

  • All Bronze membership benefits.
  • Discounts on selected events.
  • Business training opportunities.
  • Featured business promotion.

Gold Membership Benefits

Our premium membership level.

  • All Silver membership benefits.
  • Priority business spotlight placement.
  • Exclusive networking events.
  • Additional promotional opportunities.
